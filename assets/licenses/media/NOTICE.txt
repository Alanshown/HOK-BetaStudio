Build setup: put vgmstream-cli.exe, the DLLs from its release archive, and
ffmpeg.exe in .tools/media/runtime. tooling/build-desktop.ps1 copies this folder
to worker/media and the licenses to assets/licenses/media.

Decoding is performed locally. WEM export preserves source bytes; MP3 is an
actual audio conversion. This program does not encode arbitrary audio to WEM.
